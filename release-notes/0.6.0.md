Postino 0.6.0 turns the Console tab into a timeline of the whole send, lets you clean up the list of recent workspaces, and shows what changed after every update.

## What changes

### New

- **Response Console** (#194): the Console tab lists everything that happened during the last send, in order: script logs with their time and line, the network exchange (open it as a tree or as raw HTTP text) and the errors. Filter it by kind or by text, and clear it.
- **Workspace switcher** (#193): the recent workspaces show their name over their path, and each one has an X that removes it from the list. The folder on disk is never touched.
- **What's new** (#189): after an update, Postino opens a tab with the notes of the new version. Turn it off in Settings, Appearance, and open it at any time with **Show what's new** in the command palette or from About.

### Polish

- **Git log keys** (#188): after a click on a commit, the arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End move through the history and show each commit's detail.
- **Search box glow** (#190): the search box of the title bar lights up with a soft accent glow when the pointer is over it.
- **Lighter focus border** (#191): the commit message and the fields of dialogs show a thin accent border when focused, instead of a thick blue ring.
- **Section labels** in sentence case (Recent, Collections, ...) instead of uppercase, from the shared design system.
